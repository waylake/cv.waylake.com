About

I am a software engineer who likes to understand a domain and then turn it into a product. I have worked in carbon accounting, clinical trials and B2B SaaS, building software that people actually use alongside colleagues who are not developers.

What to build matters, and so does how to run it. I have designed and improved the path from a commit to a user who depends on it, and lately I focus on using AI to close the gap between people and the software they operate.
